# User Manual — Prototype Scope

1. Create a farm and growing zone.
2. Register a plant, choose its linked device, upload a real plant photo or use a local reference image, then provision that device with its own MQTT credential.
3. Confirm TEST MODE and verify telemetry reaches the dashboard.
4. Upload a plant image and wait for AI analysis.
5. Read recommendations and acknowledge alerts.
6. Only after hardware inspection, switch to MANUAL and send a bounded pump command.
7. Keep AUTO disabled until soil/plant calibration and field test acceptance are complete.
